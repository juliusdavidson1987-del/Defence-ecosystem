◎ About

The Defence Ecosystem

An interactive map of the organisations involved in defence innovation and procurement — strategy and acquisition bodies, science and innovation units, front-line commands, academia, catapults, test facilities, industry, capital, and the NATO, European, US and Five Eyes partners around them.

How to use it

Find your door — tell the router what you are, how mature your work is, your domain, what you need and where; it ranks the organisations most likely to be your way in, with an access rating and the first step. Explore — click a branch to open it and trace the connections. Search — jump to any organisation. Every node carries its website, contact emails and where its live calls and competitions are posted.

How the shortlist is scored

Each organisation is tagged with who it serves, the maturity (TRL) band it typically accepts, its domains, what it offers, its geography and how accessible it is. The router matches your inputs against those tags. The tags — especially TRL bands and access ratings — are informed estimates to help you self-qualify, not official classifications.

Currency & accuracy

Reflects the 2025 Defence Reform, the Defence Industrial Strategy 2025 and the 2026 Defence Investment Plan. It is representative, not exhaustive, and the landscape moves quickly — verify any contact, link or opportunity on the organisation's own site before acting on it.

Sharing & hosting

This is a single self-contained file. Share it by sending the file, or host it free on any static host (GitHub Pages, Netlify, Cloudflare Pages, or an internal SharePoint) to give people a link. The Share buttons build deep links that reopen a specific search or organisation. If a defence-ecosystem-data.json file is placed alongside a hosted copy, the map refreshes from it automatically — so the data can be updated without rebuilding the app.

Accessibility & views

Use the ◐ Theme button to switch between dark, light and high-contrast. ☰ Explore opens a keyboard-navigable, filterable table of every organisation as an alternative to the visual map. Contacts, opportunities and organisation facts carry a currency stamp, and you can mark any entry verified, suggest a correction, favourite it, or pin it to ⇄ Compare.
