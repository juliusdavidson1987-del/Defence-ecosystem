◎ About

The Defence Ecosystem

A shared, interactive map of the organisations involved in defence innovation and procurement across NATO and allied nations — strategy and acquisition bodies, science and innovation units, front-line commands, academia, catapults, test facilities, industry, capital, and the alliance structures that connect them. It began as a UK map and is being extended so every allied nation can be represented to the same depth; no nation is treated as the centre.

About the links

Website addresses shown as solid, clickable links have been verified to resolve. Addresses shown as plain, dashed text are a best-effort starting point that hasn't been confirmed — treat them as a name to search for rather than a guaranteed URL. This keeps the tool honest: nothing that looks clickable should lead to a dead page. If you confirm a correct address, use ✎ Suggest a correction so it can be verified and promoted to a live link.

Two ways to explore — pick your lens

Use the lens toggle in the header to re-organise the entire map. ✹ Alliance & country puts NATO, the EU and every nation as equal peers — open a country to see its bodies grouped by what they do; no nation is the trunk. ◈ Technology re-roots the same organisations by capability — AI, space, cyber, maritime, uncrewed and more — so you see who works on a problem across every allied nation at once. ⌂ UK detail keeps the original UK-structured map, which currently holds the deepest content. The same organisations appear in every lens; only the structure changes.

Make it yours — choose your starting point

On first use, pick the nation you're starting from; the map opens there and remembers it. The ◈ View dropdown lets you switch that any time — view a single nation or bloc (UK, USA, NATO, Europe, Canada, Australia, New Zealand), a function across all nations (every procurement body, every innovation accelerator, every VC, every research base, side by side), an organisation type, or a technology interest. The ⌂ Home entry returns you to your nation in one click.

How to use it

Find your door — tell the router what you are, how mature your work is, your domain, what you need and where; it ranks the organisations most likely to be your way in, with an access rating and the first step. Explore — click a branch to open it and trace the connections. Search — jump to any organisation. Every node carries its website, contact emails and where its live calls and competitions are posted.

How the shortlist is scored

Each organisation is tagged with who it serves, the maturity (TRL) band it typically accepts, its domains, what it offers, its geography and how accessible it is. The router matches your inputs against those tags. The tags — especially TRL bands and access ratings — are informed estimates to help you self-qualify, not official classifications.

Currency & accuracy

Reflects the 2025 Defence Reform, the Defence Industrial Strategy 2025 and the 2026 Defence Investment Plan. It is representative, not exhaustive, and the landscape moves quickly — verify any contact, link or opportunity on the organisation's own site before acting on it.

Sharing & hosting

This is a single self-contained file. Share it by sending the file, or host it free on any static host (GitHub Pages, Netlify, Cloudflare Pages, or an internal SharePoint) to give people a link. The Share buttons build deep links that reopen a specific search or organisation. If a defence-ecosystem-data.json file is placed alongside a hosted copy, the map refreshes from it automatically — so the data can be updated without rebuilding the app.

Accessibility & views

Use the ◐ Theme button to switch between dark, light and high-contrast. ☰ Explore opens a keyboard-navigable, filterable table of every organisation as an alternative to the visual map. Contacts, opportunities and organisation facts carry a currency stamp, and you can mark any entry verified, suggest a correction, favourite it, or pin it to ⇄ Compare.
